## Patterns

1. **Persian story + home/garden renovation sells** — Golmorad and Bagh Negar (≈6M each,
   200k+ ratings each) plus Bagh Goli, Mozafarkhan. All use **match-3**. The "restore an old
   house + romance" theme is saturated.
2. **Merge has no Persian story title.** Merge games on Bazaar/Myket are foreign and
   English-only (Merge Mansion, Mergest Kingdom, Mergedom, Journey Home, Foodstars…), while
   merge-2 is the fastest-growing casual genre worldwide (revenue +94% YoY H1 2025; Gossip
   Harbor > $650M in 2025) and Gossip Harbor is top-3 grossing on Google Play Iran.
3. **Real-prize events drive retention** (Taas o Takht, Bagh Negar, Golmorad, Menchico).
4. **Offline matters**: international cuts in 2025–2026; Bazaar billing and domestic ad
   networks keep working.
5. **Audience**: Amirza and the story-puzzle games skew female; Iranian gamers ~38% female,
   average age 29, ~96% play on mobile.

## Decision

Persian merge-2 with an ongoing neighbourhood drama («محله‌ی ما»): different mechanic from
the match-3 story games, different fantasy from the "old house" titles, built fully offline,
monetized through Bazaar billing + rewarded ads, with prize events as a later live-ops layer.
